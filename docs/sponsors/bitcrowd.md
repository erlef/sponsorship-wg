# Who is bitcrowd

The friendly team at bitcrowd is made up of hardworking and approachable experts from around the world. Working remotely and from the comfortable office in Berlin’s Mitte district (it has a great coffee machine).

They don’t simply write code and craft designs. They care about both the process and the product – helping our clients through each stage, from the project’s beginnings to long-term maintenance and post-release development.

The supportive, sustainable, and pragmatic environment at bitcrowd has provided our greatest achievement: an enduring, successful relationship with our clients.
